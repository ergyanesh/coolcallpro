⚠️ Safety First

Critical Safety Precautions

Review these safety steps before service is scheduled or a technician arrives. Your safety is the priority.

🔴

Smell Gas? Act Now

  • Do NOT turn on lights or electrical switches
  • Leave immediately and leave the door open if possible
  • Call 911 from outside
  • Then contact the 24/7 dispatch line to get connected with a local HVAC professional
View Emergency Safety Tips →
🟡

AC Not Cooling (Summer)

  • Do not open AC unit panels yourself
  • Turn the unit OFF at the thermostat
  • Keep pets and vulnerable household members cool with fans
  • Check the circuit breaker only if safe — do not touch wiring
  • Request HVAC service if the issue persists
View AC Safety Tips →
🔵

Furnace Failure (Winter)

  • Keep thermostat set at 55°F or higher to help prevent pipe freezing
  • Do NOT use a gas stove for heat
  • Never run a generator indoors
  • Watch for pipe freeze risk during extreme cold
  • Request priority winter HVAC service if needed
View Furnace Safety Tips →

These tips are for general safety awareness only. In emergencies involving gas, fire, or carbon monoxide concerns, contact emergency services (911) first.
