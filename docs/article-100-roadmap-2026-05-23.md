## Cadence + measurement protocol

**Target:** 1 article per week average (~7-10 hours per pillar / 3-5 hours per cluster article).
**Realistic timeline:** 67 articles ÷ ~3.5 articles/month = ~19 months → February 2028 to reach 100.

**Per-article workflow** (from `.agents/SKILLS/hvac-article-writer.md`):
1. Pick candidate from this menu
2. Write 3-question uniqueness test, share with user for sign-off
3. WebFetch all primary sources cited in the candidate's "Required primary sources" field
4. Add slug to cluster_map.json with status=`in_progress`
5. Draft outline + share for shape sign-off
6. Compose HTML following the conversion arc (CLAUDE.md section)
7. Verify every claim against the "No Assumptions, No Guesswork" rule (CLAUDE.md)
8. Generate hero image prompt (no thermostats / screens / phones)
9. Run `python audit_article.py --strict --browser-verified`
10. Ship → flip status to `live` → `python generate_link_plan.py` → commit + push

**Measurement protocol:**
- Every 4-6 articles shipped: pull fresh GSC export, group queries by intent theme, compare against ranking data for newly-shipped articles
- If a Phase shows weak GSC pickup, reorder remaining phases (the roadmap is a menu, not a queue)
- Track per-article: which is producing call volume in MarketCall reports, which is producing impressions but no clicks (re-evaluate H1/meta), which is producing nothing (consider archival)

**Pillar refresh cadence:** review each of the 6 pillars annually for OBBBA/AIM Act/HEAR updates + new cluster-mate articles to link. Pillars are NOT in the 67-article count above; they're already live.

## Operating principle (final)

The roadmap is a MENU, not a QUEUE. Re-pull GSC every 4-6 articles. Re-evaluate phasing if the data tells us something we didn't predict (e.g., heat-pump search volume spikes earlier than expected due to a HEAR rebate change; a refrigerant news cycle pulls forward refrigerant content). Phases are seasonally-anchored but article-level priority is data-driven within each phase.

The 3-question uniqueness test, the conversion arc, the no-guesswork rule, and the verifiability + YMYL discipline are NON-NEGOTIABLE for every article regardless of phase. Quality > velocity. One article that ranks and converts is worth ten that don't.
