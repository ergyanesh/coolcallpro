Heating Operating Cost in Little Rock: Heat Pump vs Gas Furnace

At Little Rock's state-average rates (12.7¢/kWh electricity, $1.55/therm natural gas), the heat pump wins on operating cost: $12.44/MMBtu for the heat pump vs $16.17/MMBtu for the gas furnace, a 23% margin. For a typical 1,500 sqft home in Little Rock's climate (3,053 HDD), that maps to roughly $400/year for a heat pump vs $550/year for a gas furnace at current rates — actual usage varies with insulation, thermostat habits, and equipment efficiency. The math, transparently: 1 MMBtu = 293.07 kWh = 10 therms. Heat pump cost-per-MMBtu = (electricity rate × 293.07) ÷ COP; gas furnace cost-per-MMBtu = (gas rate × 10) ÷ AFUE. Modern heat pumps achieve a coefficient of performance (COP) of 2.5 to 4 across most U.S. climates per the U.S. Department of Energy; modern gas furnaces hit 95-98% AFUE.
