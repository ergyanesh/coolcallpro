Climate & HVAC Demands in Mississippi

Mississippi spans IECC Zones 2A and 3A. With summer highs averaging 92°F, cooling is the primary demand on your HVAC system. Winter lows near 35°F mean heating needs are moderate but still important. Average electricity rates sit at 14.53¢/kWh, making system efficiency an important factor in your monthly energy costs. Learn more about heat pump options for your home.

SEER2 Efficiency Requirements in Mississippi

Mississippi falls within the Southeast SEER2 region. As of January 2023, all new central air conditioning systems installed in Mississippi must meet a minimum SEER2 rating of 14.3. When replacing your AC or heat pump, ensure your contractor installs a unit that meets or exceeds this minimum — upgrading to a higher-efficiency system can significantly reduce your energy bills. HVAC Licensing Requirements in Mississippi

Mississippi requires HVAC contractors to hold a license issued by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors. Before hiring, verify your contractor's credentials through the board's license lookup tool. Licensed contractors carry insurance, pull permits correctly, and stand behind their work. See our safety tips for more on what to verify before hiring.

Utility Companies & HVAC Rebates in Mississippi

The major utility providers in Mississippi include Entergy Mississippi, Mississippi Power (Southern Co.), TVA.

Available rebate programs:

  • Entergy MS EE Programs
  • Mississippi Power Rebates
  • TVA EnergyRight
  • Federal Tax Credits — may apply for qualifying high-efficiency installations; check energystar.gov for current amounts and eligibility

Contact your utility provider or HVAC contractor to confirm current eligibility and amounts. Learn about HVAC financing options.

HVAC Installation Costs in Mississippi

A standard central AC replacement in Mississippi typically costs $2,800–$6,500, and furnace installations run $2,200–$5,000. Costs vary by city, system size, efficiency rating, and installation complexity. Higher-efficiency units cost more upfront but lower monthly bills, especially given Mississippi's electricity rates.
