Warm-Humid Climate (Zone 3A)

Per U.S. Department of Energy Energy Saver guidance, Zone 3A warm-humid climates produce significant summer latent (moisture) loads. Variable-speed compressor systems are particularly effective in the SC Midlands’ humid summers.
