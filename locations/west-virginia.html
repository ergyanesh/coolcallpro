🌡️ Climate Demands

mixed cooling and heating demand

West Virginia spans IECC 4A, 5A. Summer highs avg 82°F, winter lows near 22°F. Average electricity 16.26¢/kWh — system efficiency directly drives monthly costs. Learn more about heat pump options.

💡 SEER2 Code

North region: SEER2 13.4 minimum

As of January 2023, all new central AC systems in West Virginia must meet SEER2 13.4. Higher-efficiency systems cost more upfront but reduce monthly bills. See our summer AC guide.

💰 Typical Costs

West Virginia HVAC installation costs

A standard central AC replacement in West Virginia typically costs $3,000–$6,500, while furnace installations run $2,800–$6,500. Costs vary by city, system size, efficiency rating, and installation complexity. See our full HVAC cost guide.

🌀️ Climate Hazards

FEMA-tracked exposure

Per FEMA's National Risk Index, West Virginia faces elevated Ice Storm, Severe Thunderstorm, and Flooding exposure. Each event stresses HVAC systems — schedule a technician inspection after major weather before restart.

📋 Licensing Requirements

Verify before you hire

West Virginia requires HVAC contractors to hold a license issued by the Division of Labor, HVAC Licensing Section. Before hiring, verify your contractor's credentials through the board's license lookup tool. Licensed contractors carry insurance, pull permits correctly, and stand behind their work.
