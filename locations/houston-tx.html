❄️ NO AC

AC out, blowing warm, or iced over

Outdoor unit silent · indoor blower running but warm air · ice on the refrigerant lines · short-cycling on/off. The most common cause is electrical (capacitor, contactor) or refrigerant — both require a technician.

🔥 NO HEAT

Furnace not igniting or blowing cold

Furnace won't ignite · blowing cold air · short-cycling · burning smell on first startup. If you smell gas, leave the building immediately and call 911 first.

💧 WATER LEAK

Water dripping from vent or air handler

Water from a ceiling vent · pooling near the indoor air handler · drain pan overflowing. The #1 cause in humid Houston summers is a clogged condensate drain line — clearing it requires working around the evaporator coil and is a technician task.

📋 ACR Contractor License

Texas TDLR — verify before hire

Every HVAC contractor in Houston must hold a current Air Conditioning and Refrigeration (ACR) Contractor license under Texas Occupations Code Chapter 1302 and Texas Administrative Code Title 16 Chapter 75. Two classes distinguished by permitted equipment size. Verify the license number before authorizing any work.

💰 Local Rebates & Permits

CenterPoint, DSIRE, Houston Permitting

Houston electricity is distributed by CenterPoint Energy (TDU); retail service comes from your chosen Retail Electric Provider under the Texas deregulated market. The CenterPoint Standard Offer Program typically pays up to $500/unit for qualifying high-efficiency equipment. Permit fees set by Houston Permitting Center (Mechanical Section, 832.394.8850); contact for the current schedule.
