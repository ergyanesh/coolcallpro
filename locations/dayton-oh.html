HVAC Licensing & Permits in Dayton, OH

Before hiring any HVAC contractor in Ohio, verify they hold the proper State License Required (OH OCILB - HVAC Contractor). Verify any contractor's license at the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board (OCILB), Dept. of Commerce.
