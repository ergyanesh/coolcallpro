Finding Trustworthy HVAC Professionals in Your Area

Regardless of your location, these safety principles apply when hiring any HVAC professional. For a complete guide, review our HVAC Safety Tips.

Before the Technician Arrives:

  • Verify their business name and request the technician's name before arrival.
  • Ensure they are arriving in a marked, branded vehicle if possible.
  • Have a clear understanding of the diagnostic fee before they dispatch to your home.

Regional Red Flags:

  • Florida & Gulf Coast: Beware of "door-to-door" AC inspectors offering free mold checks; this is often a high-pressure sales tactic.
  • Southwest: Be skeptical of contractors pushing "freon recharge" as a routine summer maintenance item; refrigerant circuits are sealed and a low charge indicates a physical leak that must be fixed.
  • Northeast: Be cautious of low-priced boiler tune-up specials that inevitably result in a recommendation for a total system replacement. Seek second opinions.
